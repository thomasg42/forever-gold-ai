What holds a business back

It is rarely the work. It is the bookkeeping that does not talk to the rest of the business. The receipt in the truck. The quote you meant to send. The customer who called twice and got two different answers.

Things fall through the cracks because the only place they all meet is you. Every new hire, every new tool and every busy week means explaining the business again.

An AI consultant fixes the system, not the people. Thomas comes in, finds what is holding the business back, and cleans it up.

What AI consulting covers

Seven piles of work. One system.

Invoices and receipts

Every invoice and receipt in one place, tied to the job and the customer, instead of the truck, the inbox and a shoebox.

Quotes

Quotes drafted from your job notes and held for your approval before they go out.

Customer records

One record per customer: what they asked for, what you quoted, what they paid. Nobody retypes a name.

Calls and leads

Calls answered in your business name, every new lead followed up, and the record kept on the customer.

Scheduling and jobs

Bookings that check the slot before they confirm, with the job and the close-out in the same place.

Crew and paperwork

Clock-ins, checklists, time sheets and signed agreements on a phone instead of a pile on the seat.

The numbers

The numbers you choose to watch, on one screen, from the same records the rest of the business uses.
