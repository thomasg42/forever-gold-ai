Voice AI

AI Receptionist That Answers in Your Business's Name

Picture a Tuesday morning when you are already with a customer and the shop line rings. You cannot pick up. The caller hears a greeting you recorded years ago, leaves a short voicemail, and goes back to looking for someone who can take the job today. By the time you listen, the work has often moved on.

An AI receptionist is the person who stays by that phone. It speaks in your business name, uses the greeting you approved, and answers from the facts you gave it. It is not a generic robot reading a script from another industry. The words are yours. The hours it covers are the hours you choose.

Owners tell us the same scene. They are on a roof, under a truck, in a kitchen, or driving between jobs. The phone is in a pocket. A missed call is not a small annoyance. It is a person who was ready to book and then called the next name. The receptionist exists so that moment does not depend on you being free.

How it works

  1. A call comes in.
  2. The AI answers with your greeting and your answers.
  3. It books, takes a message, or transfers to you.
  4. You get the summary by text or email.
  5. Anything it is unsure about comes to you.

What you can change

Adjustable

Change the greeting, the answers, the booking rules, and the hours without waiting on a rebuild. If you stop taking a kind of job, say so and the next call follows the new rule.

Fixable

If a call fails to connect or a summary does not arrive, the failure raises an alert to Thomas. You are not left guessing why the phone went quiet.

Flexible

Some businesses want every caller offered a time. Some want a message and a callback from a person. The path bends around the way you already decide.

Customizable

Your services, your service area, and the questions you ask a caller are written for your shop. Shelf menus from another trade stay out of it.

When something goes wrong

If the voice system is unsure, the call comes to you. If a transfer fails, the caller can leave a message and you still get the summary by text or email. The fallback is a person, not a dead end.

What it connects to

The receptionist can sit with Google Workspace and Gmail for the summary, Twilio for the phone path, ElevenLabs for the voice, n8n for the handoff, and Cloudflare in front of the site that feeds it. You do not have to live in ten tabs to hear what happened.

Fits at stage

This is a Start system. It still matters when you have a crew, because the phone should not follow you up a ladder. At Scale it keeps one front door for more than one crew.

Questions

Will it invent answers?

No. It uses the answers you approved. Anything outside that list comes to you.

Can I hear what it said?

Yes. You get a summary by text or email after the call.

Do I have to be technical?

No. You approve the words. We set the path.

A plumber on a crawlspace job does not need a second pair of hands on the phone. The receptionist takes the greeting you wrote, offers the times you allow, and sends you a short note: who called, what they needed, and whether a time was booked. If the caller asks for a job you do not do, the note says so and the caller is not promised a visit. That is the whole product. It is adjustable when your season changes, fixable when a carrier hiccup drops a call, flexible when you want messages instead of bookings on Fridays, and custom to the way you already talk to customers. The Goldie slot on this page is where our own receptionist will sit later. Until then, call or text Thomas and the same phone number on this site reaches a person.

Think through a single afternoon. At 2 the line rings while you are writing a measurement. The receptionist answers, asks the address and the problem, and books a window you marked open. At 2:10 you feel the phone buzz with the summary. You finish the measurement. You do not play voicemail in the truck at 7. That is time returned to the job you were already paid to do, and it is a caller who did not have to try the next company. The system does not claim it catches every human situation. It claims a clear path: answer, book or message, summarize, and hand you the unsure ones.
