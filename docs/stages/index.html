Stages

Succeed at any stage.

Start, Grow, and Scale are a sequence. Build the front door before you build the dashboard.

Start

Only you

Signs you are here

You answer the phone, write the quotes, and send the invoices yourself. The business moves when you move.

What breaks first

Calls go to voicemail while you are on a job. Leads sit until evening.

What to build first

AI receptionist, Speed-to-Lead, and a website.

Why the next stage gets easier

Those three keep the front door covered, so a crew can be added without you still catching every call.

Grow

A crew

Signs you are here

People are on jobs, and you are still the one booking, invoicing, and checking the schedule.

What breaks first

Two people get offered the same slot. Invoices wait until night.

What to build first

Booking, job to invoice, and team apps, on top of the Start systems.

Why the next stage gets easier

The day has a calendar and a close-out, so another crew can follow the same pattern.

Scale

Crews or locations

Signs you are here

More than one crew, or more than one place, and the numbers live in separate tabs.

What breaks first

Paperwork piles up. Nobody can see the week on one screen.

What to build first

Documents, AI operations, and a custom assistant, on top of what you already run.

Why the next stage gets easier

The same checks and alerts cover the next location. You are not rebuilding the company by hand.

Which stage are you in?
