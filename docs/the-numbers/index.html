How to read these numbers

The Census figure and the Thryv figure measure different things. One counts production use across U.S. firms. The other counts any use among surveyed small businesses. Read them side by side. Do not fold them into one number.

The callback figures come from one vendor's customers. They describe missed calls in that set.
